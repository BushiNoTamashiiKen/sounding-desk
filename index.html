Roberto Clemente digital bobbleheads arrive for Clemente Day.

Datavault AI · 15 Sep 2026

Two editions, Universal and Signature, are offered from 15 September through 29 September 2026 at day.21inright.com. The date is Roberto Clemente Day and the first day of National Hispanic Heritage Month.

21 In Right, the family entity that manages his name, image, and likeness, offers the collectibles. The company’s note says the work uses Data Vault, DataValue, DataScore, and the Information Data Exchange.

This activation puts his name, image, and likeness to work on the family’s terms, with every collectible authorized by 21 In Right and delivered through Datavault AI technology.

Nathaniel T. Bradley, chief executive, in the 15 September 2026 note
